How to use

  1. Enable Dual Subtitle Companion in Safari Settings → Extensions.
  2. Allow access to netflix.com.
  3. Open a playback page that offers both English and Traditional Chinese subtitles.
  4. Click the extension button in the Safari toolbar.
  5. Enable bilingual subtitles. Disable them to restore the previous website subtitle track.

Requirements and limitations

  • Currently supported and tested only on macOS Safari.
  • A valid account and subscription for the supported service are required.
  • Subtitle availability varies by title, episode, profile, account language, and region.
  • The extension relies on an undocumented player implementation and may stop working after website updates.
  • Initial subtitle detection can take several seconds.
  • Safari native video fullscreen cannot display a webpage subtitle overlay.
  • The extension does not translate, download, export, or redistribute subtitles, video, or audio.
